📊 Executive Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 4 years of retail sales data (2010–2013) to evaluate revenue growth, profitability, regional performance, and product efficiency.

🔍 Project Overview
This dashboard transforms raw transactional data into executive-level insights, focusing on:
- Total Sales & Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Regional performance comparison
- Product-level profitability analysis

📈 Key Insights
- Strong sales growth from 2010 to 2013
- Profit growth lags behind revenue growth, indicating margin pressure
- Central region delivers highest profitability
- One high-selling product generates negative profit

🛠 Skills & Tools
- Power BI
- Data Modeling
- DAX (CALCULATE, DIVIDE)
- KPI Design
- Profitability & Margin Analysis
- Data Storytelling
